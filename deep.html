Model files

Saved models

The notebook shows the preprocessing needed to use these models and also includes the Basic MLP experiment.

scikit-learn

Logistic Regression

The baseline classifier estimates churn probability from the prepared features. It achieved a test ROC-AUC of 0.8352.

Download modellogistic_regression.joblib ↓
Keras

Basic MLP

The first neural network tested. It uses two hidden layers with 32 and 16 neurons and serves as the reference for the later MLP variants.

Download modelmlp_basic.keras ↓
Keras

MLP with Batch Normalization

Adds batch normalization before the ReLU activation in each hidden layer to test its effect on training and validation performance.

Download modelmlp_bn.keras ↓
Keras

MLP with Dropout

Uses 20% dropout after each hidden layer during training as an attempt to reduce overfitting.

Download modelmlp_drop.keras ↓
Keras

MLP with L2 Regularization

Applies an L2 penalty of 0.001 to the weights of both hidden layers to discourage large weights.

Download modelmlp_l2.keras ↓
Keras

Residual MLP

Adds a projected shortcut from the first hidden layer to the second layer's output before the final prediction.

Download modelmlp_res.keras ↓
KerasTuner

Tuned MLP

Random search selected a network with 64 and 16 hidden units. Among the four models tested, it had the highest F1 score (0.6101) and recall (0.5963).

Download modelmlp_tuned.keras ↓
scikit-learn

Random Forest

An ensemble of 200 decision trees used to compare a nonlinear tree-based approach with the neural networks on the validation set.

Download modelrandom_forest.joblib ↓
Gradient boosting

XGBoost

A boosted tree classifier that achieved the highest test ROC-AUC (0.8381) among the four models evaluated on the test set.
